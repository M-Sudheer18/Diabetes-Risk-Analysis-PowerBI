# Diabetes Insights Dashboard 📊

## Overview

The **Diabetes Insights Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze diabetes-related healthcare data and uncover meaningful patterns that support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into diabetes prevalence, patient demographics, glucose levels, pregnancy history, and high-risk patient groups. Through intuitive visualizations and KPIs, healthcare professionals and analysts can better understand factors associated with diabetes and identify patients who may require early intervention.

---

# Project Objectives

* Analyze the prevalence of diabetes within the patient population.
* Identify age groups most affected by diabetes.
* Examine the relationship between glucose levels and diabetes occurrence.
* Study the impact of pregnancy history on diabetes risk.
* Discover high-risk patient profiles for targeted healthcare interventions.
* Present findings through interactive and easy-to-understand visualizations.

---

# Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Average Age of Diabetic Patients
* Total Pregnancies among Diabetic Patients
* Diabetes Percentage
* Non-Diabetes Percentage
* Total Diabetic Patients

### Visualizations Included

1. Diabetes Cases by Age
2. Diabetes per Glucose Level
3. Total Pregnancies (Diabetes) by Pregnancies
4. Diabetes Distribution Pie Chart
5. Non-Diabetes Percentage Donut Chart
6. Non-Diabetic Patients by Pregnancies
7. Dynamic Age Filter
8. Summary Report Section

# Dataset Attributes

The analysis is based on healthcare-related patient records containing features such as:

| Feature                    | Description                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Pregnancies                | Number of pregnancies                            |
| Glucose                    | Plasma glucose concentration                     |
| Blood Pressure             | Diastolic blood pressure                         |
| Skin Thickness             | Triceps skin fold thickness                      |
| Insulin                    | Serum insulin level                              |
| BMI                        | Body Mass Index                                  |
| Diabetes Pedigree Function | Genetic diabetes likelihood                      |
| Age                        | Patient age                                      |
| Outcome                    | Diabetes Status (0 = Non-Diabetic, 1 = Diabetic) |

# Dashboard Insights

## 1. Diabetes Prevalence

Approximately **38.91%** of patients are diabetic, while **61.09%** are non-diabetic.

### Interpretation

A significant portion of the population is affected by diabetes, emphasizing the need for early diagnosis and preventive healthcare programs.

---

## 2. Average Age of Diabetic Patients

The average age of diabetic patients is **37.07 years**.

### Interpretation

Diabetes is commonly observed among middle-aged adults, indicating that the disease affects individuals during their productive years.

---

## 3. Glucose is the Strongest Risk Factor

Patients with elevated glucose levels are considerably more likely to be diabetic.

### Interpretation

Glucose level demonstrates the strongest correlation with diabetes occurrence and serves as a critical indicator for early diagnosis.

---

## 4. Pregnancy History and Diabetes

Diabetes cases are more concentrated among patients with higher pregnancy counts, particularly between **6 and 10 pregnancies**.

### Interpretation

Pregnancy history may contribute to increased diabetes risk and should be considered during patient screening.

---

## 5. High-Risk Patient Group

The highest-risk patients typically exhibit:

* Age between 30 and 50 years
* Elevated glucose levels
* Multiple pregnancies

### Interpretation

These individuals should be prioritized for diabetes screening and preventive healthcare measures.
